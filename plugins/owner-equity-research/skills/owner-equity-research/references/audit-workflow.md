# Read-only audit workflow

Bind the audit to an exact commit/tree or archive byte set. Do not edit, repair, reacquire evidence,
or publish during the audit.

## Research and price-blind boundary

- Verify public Schemas, immutable contract reconstruction, canonical serialization,
  fingerprints, dependency closure, cutoff, issuer, period/unit/currency/scope, and dangling edges.
- Verify official-source and named-human decision boundaries. Confirm language-model output cannot
  directly create Facts, final Decisions, or CalculationResults.
- Verify ordinary research and all price-blind preparation contain no target-security price,
  market capitalization, valuation request/result, brokerage account access, or network quote.
- Verify ResearchBundle/RunManifest and price-blind artifacts reload from exact canonical bytes;
  protected McKinsey and Penman hashes never change after freeze.

## Futu, request, execution, and archive

- Verify explicit valuation intent precedes every Futu call. Replay signed Legal, Account
  Entitlement, Supply Chain, Runtime Isolation, and Security Identity receipts; the protocol
  allowlist; `qotLogined=true`; `trdLogined=false`; private encrypted CAS receipts; SEC/IR-primary
  cross-checks; and the governed daily-close adapter. Reject trade/account protocols and manual,
  scraped, simulated, free-API, or caller-authored prices.
- Verify current-share event identity and exactly-once arithmetic, legal-name/provider provenance,
  append-only FactLedger preservation, canonical sorted assumptions, exact numeric projection, and
  separate McKinsey/Penman panels.
- Verify wheel/runtime/component identities, no-network execution, parent-side Schema validation,
  exact canonical stdout, and one kernel call.
- Verify the output directory contains exactly the registered six files. Mutate every member and
  prove reload fails. Recompute file hashes, protected hashes, Snapshot/Handoff/request/result
  fingerprints, receipts, and deterministic archive ID independently.
- Verify blocked and specialist outcomes retain no archive or kernel bytes.

## Synthesis, score, report, and Publisher

- Verify McKinsey comes only from the strict kernel result; forward Penman ReOI is an independent
  `PROJECT_EXTENSION`; comparables contain 5-15 named-human reviewed peers with at least five valid
  peers and two multiples. Both composite medians require all three compatible panels and no
  weights; dispersion above 50% is `contested`.
- Verify each Graham/Buffett/Munger/Duan lens has exactly five 0-20 components, Unknown/partial is
  null rather than zero, and Score bytes cannot change Facts, assumptions, panel eligibility, or
  any valuation fingerprint.
- Verify report profile, 30-60 page extractable-Chinese PDF, renderer/font/license identities,
  Python-produced tables/charts, and ReportBuildReceipt. Research-only output has no market or
  target price.
- Verify Publisher consumes only strict reloads, retains exact six-file bytes, enforces 512 members
  and 512 MiB, uses no-follow staging/fsync/0444/0555/atomic rename, and reloads its root manifest.

## Release and severity

- Classify findings P0, P1, P2, or P3 with exact file/line or byte evidence.
- Exact-head implementation acceptance, a release candidate, and a stable release all require
  P0=P1=P2=P3=0. RC or stable release additionally requires the applicable CI and independent
  fresh-context evidence.
- Confirm only the primary research Skill permits implicit invocation. Explicit valuation must be
  user-triggered; explicit audit remains read-only.
- Confirm no weighted target price, Score-driven valuation, raw licensed Futu bytes, trading
  connector, account mutation, or external Publisher action entered the release.
