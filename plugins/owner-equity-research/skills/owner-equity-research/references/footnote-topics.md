# Footnote topics

Represent every topic with exactly one of `reviewed`, `not_disclosed`, `not_applicable`, or
`blocked`:

- revenue recognition and contract balances;
- stock-based compensation;
- leases;
- income taxes;
- pensions and postretirement benefits;
- goodwill, intangibles, and impairment;
- debt, liquidity, and covenants;
- contingencies and litigation;
- acquisitions and divestitures;
- restructuring and exit costs;
- related parties;
- commitments, guarantees, and off-balance-sheet obligations;
- supplier finance;
- variable-interest entities;
- fair value, derivatives, and hedging.

Discover additional material Note headings and add them as `dynamic`; do not replace a mandatory
topic with a dynamic review. `reviewed` needs evidence, `not_applicable` needs a Claim, and
`blocked` needs a concrete missing-evidence record. Record the counterevidence search for every
topic.
