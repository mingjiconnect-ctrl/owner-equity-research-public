# Management Commitment compiler

Compile a Commitment only from a human-confirmed, measurable Statement whose reviewed Candidate
provides the same target Facts, metric components, scope, and measurement basis. Select one policy
from the versioned registry and require its exact target roles, baseline evidence, and comparison
direction.

Use one explicit due date or resolve one controlled relative fiscal deadline from a unique reported
`FiscalPeriod`. Never infer a calendar-year end for a non-calendar issuer. A KPI rename or
definition change requires an assumption-free deterministic bridge before compilation.

Narrative-only Statements remain Statements or Claims and produce no blocked Commitment. Record
`statement_not_measurable` in the run manifest. Withdrawals and supersessions are lifecycle changes;
they do not imply that the original target was missed. Phase 4B-2 does not evaluate Outcomes.
