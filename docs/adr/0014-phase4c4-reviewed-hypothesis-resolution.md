# ADR 0014: Reviewed analytical Claims and deterministic hypothesis status

Status: accepted for Phase 4C-4 development

Analytical language-model output stops at a Candidate. Only a named human decision over the exact
Candidate fingerprint and evidence-graph hash can emit a Claim. A confirmed Claim must preserve
direct target-company Fact support, the reviewed statement, counterevidence search, confidence,
and falsification condition.

Competitive-advantage status is program output, not an analyst field. The resolver and
ContractGraph apply the same priority: blocked, falsified, contested, supported, then proposed.
Supported requires the three distinct positive Claims, every mechanism support and counter role,
target official and independent authoritative evidence, complete material scope, resolved
counterevidence, and no single-indicator shortcut. A falsifying disposition requires a reviewed
falsification Claim. Comparable successors cannot delete predecessor counterevidence.

This layer produces evidence states, not a score, quality grade, valuation, target price,
recommendation, report, PDF, Publisher output, or investment conclusion.
